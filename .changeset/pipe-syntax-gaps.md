Support the rest of Druid's pipe syntax: the `|> UNION [ALL | DISTINCT] (...)`, `|> JOIN`, `|> AS`, `|> DISTINCT` and `|> RENAME` operators (`SqlUnionPipeOperator`, `SqlJoinPipeOperator`, `SqlAsPipeOperator`, `SqlDistinctPipeOperator`, `SqlRenamePipeOperator`), `|> SELECT DISTINCT` / `ALL`, order suffixes on the items of `|> AGGREGATE` (`COUNT(*) AS c DESC GROUP BY a ASC NULLS LAST`, where `ASC` / `DESC` used to be read as an alias), `WITH ... FROM` (and `WITH ... TABLE` / `VALUES`), and queries in parentheses followed by more of the statement (`(FROM t) UNION ALL (FROM u) |> ...`, the new `SqlParensQuery`). `unpipe()` converts all of them. `SqlFromQuery` now holds a whole FROM clause, so it takes aliases, joins, comma separated tables and sub-queries: its `table` and table methods (`getTableName`, `changeTable`, ...) are replaced by `fromClause` and FROM clause methods (`getFromExpressions`, `changeFromExpressions`, `getJoins`, `addJoin`, ...). `SqlOrderByExpression` is now an `SqlExpression`. Pipe operators after a `UNION ALL` now apply to the whole union rather than its last query, and a `WITH` in front of the starting query takes in the operators too (the new `SqlPipesQuery.withClause`), so they can use its tables; `unpipe()` keeps that scoping. A `UNION ALL` after pipe operators (`FROM t |> WHERE x UNION ALL FROM u`) no longer parses.
